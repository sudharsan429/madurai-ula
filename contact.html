TRAVELLER ESSENTIALS

Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries about visiting temples, cloakrooms, photography, and dress codes.

What is the dress code for temples?

Traditional attire is strictly enforced at Meenakshi Amman Temple and major shrines. Men should wear dhoti, veshti with shirt, or formal trousers with shirt. Women should wear sarees, half-sarees, or churidar with dupatta. Shorts, lungis, and sleeveless tops are not allowed inside temple sanctums.

Where can I safely store shoes and phones?

Meenakshi Amman Temple operates dedicated official shoe-keeping counters and digital locker rooms outside all four gopuram entrances (South, West, East, North). Mobile phones and smart electronic devices are prohibited inside the sanctum and must be deposited in the official electronic lockers.

What are the best hours for Darshan?

Early mornings between 5:30 AM – 7:30 AM and afternoons around 4:00 PM – 5:30 PM are the best times to experience tranquil worship with shorter waiting times. Mid-morning (10 AM – 12 PM) and festive evenings can be crowded. Online Special Entry Darshan passes (₹100) are available via TN HR&CE portal.

How do I get around Madurai?

Within the central old town, walking, cycle-rickshaws, and auto-rickshaws are the most practical way to navigate the concentric temple streets. For longer trips across the district (Keeladi, Alagar Kovil, Thirupparankundram, Athisayam), app-based cabs (Uber/Ola) and local buses from Periyar and Mattuthavani operate regularly.
