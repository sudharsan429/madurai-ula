SIX GUIDING ACTION PILLARS

🌿 The Madurai CSR & Community Framework

Aligned with UN Sustainable Development Goals

Rather than viewing CSR as a static paragraph or corporate obligation, our portal turns CSR into an active, functional feature across every food dish, monument, stay, and traditional market in Madurai.

🏪
Pillar 1 · Economic Justice

Support Local Businesses

Championing generational family messes, roadside tiffin carts, spice merchants in Avani Moola, and neighbourhood tea stalls over corporate chain franchises.

  • Direct UPI/Cash payments eliminate 25–30% aggregator platform commissions
  • Sustains local agrarian supply chains (country rice, unrefined cane jaggery, cold-pressed oils)
  • Protects micro-entrepreneurs against retail inflation
View Local Merchant Guilds →
🏛️
Pillar 2 · Living Antiquity

Protect Madurai Heritage

Safeguarding 2,500-year-old Sangam-era urban layouts, 14 carved gopurams at Meenakshi Temple, Thirumalai Nayakkar Palace, and Jain cavern inscriptions at Samanar Malai.

  • Advocating respectful heritage trail walking and zero graffiti on granite monuments
  • Collaborating with local historians to document undocumented Pandya inscriptions
  • Preserving the concentric lotus street layout (Chithirai, Avani Moola, Masi, Veli)
Explore Protected Heritage Sites →
🎨
Pillar 3 · Fair Trade Crafts

Support Local Artisans

Direct market access for GI-tagged Madurai Sungudi handloom saree weavers, Vilachery terracotta clay sculptors, and brass idol casters in Puthu Mandapam.

  • Eliminates exploitative middlemen by publishing direct workshop GPS coordinates
  • Supports traditional botanical vat dyeing and hand-tied knot techniques
  • Preserves Navarathri clay Golu doll traditions in Vilachery village
Meet Verified Artisans →
🤝
Pillar 4 · Cultural Respect

Responsible Tourism

Educating pilgrims and global explorers on sacred space sanctity, traditional temple dress codes, mobile phone lockers, and empathetic community interaction.

  • Strict observance of temple decorum and designated footwear deposit points
  • Ethical crowd navigation during Chithirai Thiruvizha & Alanganallur Jallikattu
  • Zero intrusive photography in inner sanctums and residential agraharams
Take the Responsible Pledge →
🌿
Pillar 5 · River & Eco-Sanitation

Clean Madurai

Active involvement in Vaigai River cleanup drives, Swachh Madurai temple corridor sanitation, and complete phase-out of single-use plastics around water bodies.

  • 100% banana leaf dining advocacy across all featured messes and tiffin centers
  • Promotion of reusable cloth bags (manjappai) for flower and spice shopping
  • Community trash disposal points along holy temple theppakulam tanks
View Clean Travel Guide →
🚲
Pillar 6 · Low-Carbon Mobility

Eco-Friendly Travel

Encouraging walkable heritage circuits, electric auto-rickshaws, shared public transit buses, and suburban trains to explore surrounding rural taluks with minimal carbon footprint.

  • Concentric Masi Street walking trails minimize vehicular pollution in temple zones
  • Direct recommendations for shared electric battery rickshaws (e-autos)
  • Passenger train schedules connecting Madurai Junction to Sholavandan & Usilampatti

GREEN MOBILITY & SLOW TRAVEL

Eco-Friendly Travel Guide across Madurai

Explore Madurai's sacred quarters with minimal ecological disruption. Here are tested low-carbon itineraries and conscious travel tips:

🚶 The Concentric Lotus Walk (2.4 km)

Experience the Sangam city on foot. Start from Meenakshi East Gopuram → Puthu Mandapam → Avani Moola spice market → West Masi Street parotta corners → Kazimar Mosque. Zero carbon footprint and total cultural immersion.

Best time: 6:00 AM – 9:00 AM or 5:30 PM – 8:30 PM

🛺 Shared Electric Autos (E-Rickshaws)

Over 300 battery-operated green e-rickshaws operate between Madurai Junction railway station, Periyar Bus Stand, and the 4 Veli Streets. Fare is typically ₹15–₹30 for short hops, cutting noise and smoke emissions.

Available at: Periyar Station & Simmakkal Junction

🚂 Suburban Rail to Rural Taluks

Instead of private highway taxis, take comfortable Southern Railway passenger trains from Madurai Junction to Sholavandan (banana farms), Thirumangalam, or Samayanallur for under ₹20 per passenger.

Departures every 45–60 mins from Madurai Junction
