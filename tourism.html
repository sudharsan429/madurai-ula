Tourism in Madurai

LIVING HERITAGE & SPECTACLES

Culture & Festivals of Madurai

Tamil Nadu Tourism lists the Chithirai Festival, Float Festival, and Jallikattu among Madurai's major cultural attractions, drawing millions of visitors from across the globe.

GRAND CARNIVAL

Chithirai Festival

Tamil Month of Chithirai (April – May)

One of Madurai's most important cultural and religious celebrations, uniting Shaivite and Vaishnavite traditions with majestic wooden temple chariots, street processions, and ecstatic citywide devotion.

Meenakshi Sundareswarar Alagar Processions Temple ceremonies
SACRED NUPTIALS

Meenakshi Thirukalyanam

10th Day of Chithirai Festival

The celestial wedding of Goddess Meenakshi and Lord Sundareswarar. A major highlight of the Chithirai celebrations, attended by hundreds of thousands of devotees who renew their marriage vows.

Divine Wedding Silk & Jewels Sacred Ceremony
RIVERSIDE SPECTACLE

Alagar Vaigai River Event

Chitra Pournami (Full Moon)

A major festival event involving Lord Kallazhagar's traditional journey from the Azhagar Hills mounted on a golden horse, entering the sacred waters of the river Vaigai amidst a sea of cheering devotees.

Vaigai River Golden Horse Alagar Entry
ILLUMINATED WATERS

Float Festival / Teppam

Thai Poosam (January – February)

Associated with the Mariamman Teppakulam. The deities of Meenakshi and Sundareswarar are drawn on a brightly illuminated floating raft (teppam) adorned with thousands of flowers and lamps across the 16-acre tank.

Teppakulam Illuminated Raft Island Mandapam
TAMIL VALOUR

Jallikattu

Mattu Pongal Season (Mid-January)

A major traditional Tamil cultural event celebrated with thunderous passion across the Madurai region, held at legendary historic arenas in Alanganallur, Palamedu, and Avaniyapuram.

Pongal Season Alanganallur Palamedu Avaniyapuram

NEIGHBOURHOOD DIRECTORY & WHAT EACH AREA IS FAMOUS FOR

Madurai Areas — What Each Area Is Known For

Understand Madurai district's unique character by exploring its distinct quarters, heritage streets, modern retail nodes, and archaeological sites.

Madurai Main

Meenakshi Temple, historic streets, jasmine flower markets, and ancient core.

Mahal Area

Thirumalai Nayakkar Mahal, Indo-Saracenic pillars, light & sound show, and street snacks.

Masi Streets

Temple streets + shopping + food; Madurai Sungudi sarees, brassware, and tiffin.

Simmakkal

Traditional food + markets; Kari dosa, hot mutton bone soup, parotta, and night stalls.

Town Hall Road

Restaurants + shopping; Vegetarian tiffin, sweet shops, clothing, and everyday bustle.

Vilakkuthoon

Food + commercial streets; Historic 1840 lamp post, chilled Jigarthanda, and tailoring.

Teppakulam

Teppakulam + evening food; 16-acre temple tank, float festival, and lakeside strolls.

Tallakulam

Messes + restaurants; Famous Chandran Mess, kola urundai, and biryani houses.

Anna Nagar

Modern restaurants + shopping; Zaitoon Middle Eastern grills, cafes, and upscale avenues.

KK Nagar

Restaurants + hotels; Madurai Kitchen, family restaurants, and commercial centres.

Goripalayam

Historic Dargah + food + commercial area; Sulthan parotta and buzzing street life.

Avaniyapuram

Restaurants + airport-side area; Jallikattu arena, Ayyan restaurant, and airport corridor.

Mattuthavani

Integrated bus terminal + hotels + Central wholesale flower and produce market.

Thirupparankundram

Murugan Temple + heritage; First Arupadaiveedu, rock-cut shrines, and Jain cave beds.

Azhagar Kovil

Lord Kallazhagar Temple + forested hills + Pazhamudircholai Murugan shrine.

Keelakuyilkudi

Samanar Hills (Samanar Malai); 2,000-year-old rock beds, Jain bas-reliefs, and lotus pond.

Keeladi

Archaeology; 2,600-year-old Sangam-era urban civilization excavation and modern museum.
